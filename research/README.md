# Sagar OS Long-Term Research (`research/`)

This directory is dedicated to forward-looking research, theoretical studies, architectural simulations, and whitepapers.

## Quarantined Research Areas
- `kernel/`: Exploration of custom capability-based microkernel architectures, formal verification, and POSIX compatibility layers.

## Governance & Safety Rule
> [!CAUTION]
> Nothing inside `research/` is linked to or used in the production Sagar OS Debian 13 runtime.
> This area is strictly an isolated research environment for Phase 5 exploration.
